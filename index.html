2. 信息如何存储与保护

  • 存储位置：安卓版保存在应用私有目录 /data/data/com.calorietracker.app/files/data/，其中 ct.db 为记录数据库，config.json 为配置（含明文 API Key）。其他应用无法读取该目录。电脑版保存在程序目录下的 data/ 文件夹。
  • 不会上传到开发者：开发者没有服务器，也没有任何后台上传通道。
  • 本地服务：安卓版会在设备本机启动一个内部服务（仅监听 127.0.0.1 回环地址，端口 8787），用于界面与本地数据库通信。该端口不对局域网或互联网开放。
  • 保留期限：数据一直保留在您的设备上，直到您自行删除。卸载应用会删除全部数据。
  • 安全提示：API Key 以明文形式保存在上述配置文件中。请勿将 data/ 目录或导出文件分享给他人；请为设备设置锁屏密码。

3. 信息如何对外提供（重要）

这是本应用唯一的对外数据传输通道，由您主动配置并触发：

  • 当您点击「AI 估算饮食」「AI 估算运动」「让 AI 估算 BMR」「生成报告（含 AI 分析）」或「测试连接」时，应用会把完成该任务所需的身体数据、您输入的描述文字、以及（若您选择了图片）图片本身，通过网络发送到您在「设置 → LLM 档案」中自行填写的接口地址。
  • 如果您在「设置 → 联网搜索」中启用了搜索服务，应用会把搜索关键词发送到您配置的搜索服务。
  • 应用内置若干常见服务商的接口地址模板（例如 OpenAI 兼容接口、Anthropic、Google Gemini 等形态），但只有您自行填写凭据并主动触发时才会发生数据传输；开发者不代为转发、不缓存、不留存这些数据。
  • 该第三方服务的运营者、其数据存储位置、保留期限与隐私做法，由该服务的提供方决定，不由本应用或开发者控制。若您填写的接口位于境外，上述数据将构成个人信息出境，请您自行确认该服务已完成相应合规手续，并阅读其隐私政策。
  • 若您未配置任何接口，应用不会发送任何数据，此时 AI 相关功能不可用，但手动记录与热量差计算仍可正常使用。
  • 除此之外，我们不会向任何第三方出售、出租、共享或公开您的个人信息；除法律法规要求或司法机关、监管部门依法定程序调取外，不对外提供。

请特别注意：为保护您的健康数据，建议只填写以 https:// 开头的接口地址。使用 http:// 明文地址时，数据与 API Key 可能在传输途中被他人截获。本应用已默认禁止明文网络请求，仅在您显式填写 http:// 地址时才会放行，并会向您提示风险。

Calorie Tracker is a fully local personal calorie and exercise logging tool. The developer operates no server and does not collect, upload, or store any of your personal information.

The body data and food/exercise records you enter stay in your own device's private app storage. The only network activity is sending data to the third-party AI (LLM) endpoint or search service that you configure yourself in Settings. Please read Section 3 carefully.

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics, push, or crash-reporting SDK.

2. How information is stored and protected

  • Location: on Android, in the app's private directory /data/data/com.calorietracker.app/files/data/, where ct.db holds records and config.json holds settings (including the API key in plain text). Other apps cannot read this directory. On desktop it is the data/ folder next to the program.
  • Never uploaded to the developer: the developer has no server and no upload channel.
  • Local service: on Android the app runs an internal service bound to 127.0.0.1:8787 only, for communication between the UI and the local database. It is not exposed to the LAN or the internet.
  • Retention: data stays on your device until you delete it. Uninstalling the app removes all data.
  • Security note: the API key is stored in plain text in the config file. Do not share the data/ folder or export files, and set a device lock screen.

3. Disclosure to third parties (important)

This is the app's only outbound data channel, and it is configured and triggered by you:

  • When you tap "AI-estimate food", "AI-estimate exercise", "estimate BMR", "generate report (with AI analysis)" or "test connection", the app sends the body data, your description text and (if you selected images) the images themselves over the network to the endpoint you entered under Settings → LLM profiles.
  • If you enable a search provider under Settings → Web search, the app sends the search query to the service you configured.
  • The app ships template endpoint addresses for common providers (e.g. OpenAI-compatible, Anthropic, Google Gemini forms), but no data is transmitted unless you enter credentials and trigger the action yourself. The developer does not relay, cache or retain this data.
  • That provider's operator, storage location, retention period and privacy practices are determined by that provider, not by this app or its developer. If the endpoint you enter is outside your country, this constitutes a cross-border transfer — please verify that provider's compliance and read its privacy policy.
  • If you configure no endpoint, the app sends nothing. AI features are unavailable, but manual logging and calorie-balance calculation still work.
  • Otherwise we never sell, rent, share or disclose your personal information to any third party; disclosure occurs only where required by law or by a competent authority acting through due legal process.

Important: to protect your health data, prefer endpoints beginning with https://. With a plain http:// endpoint, your data and API key may be intercepted in transit. The app blocks cleartext traffic by default and only allows it when you explicitly enter an http:// address, after warning you of the risk.
